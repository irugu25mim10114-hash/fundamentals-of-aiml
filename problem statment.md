Problem Statement
Traditional recruitment and job-search processes require manual analysis of resumes and job descriptions, which is time-consuming, inefficient, and difficult to scale when dealing with a large number of candidates and job opportunities. Furthermore, simple keyword-based resume screening may fail to identify candidates whose skills are expressed using different terms but have similar meanings.

The proposed project, “AI-Based Resume Screening and Job Recommendation System Using Transfer Learning and Natural Language Processing,” aims to develop an intelligent system that automatically analyzes resume content and matches it with relevant job descriptions.

The system will use Natural Language Processing (NLP) and a pre-trained transformer model to convert resumes and job descriptions into meaningful numerical representations (embeddings). Semantic similarity will then be calculated between the resume and available jobs to rank and recommend the most suitable opportunities.

Main Problem
To develop an AI-based system that can automatically understand the skills and information present in a candidate's resume and recommend suitable job positions by using Transfer Learning, NLP, and semantic similarity techniques.

Key challenges addressed
Manual resume screening is time-consuming.
Keyword-based matching may miss semantically similar skills.
Candidates may not know which jobs best match their skills.
Recruiters need an efficient method to screen large numbers of resumes.
Job descriptions and resumes contain unstructured text.
The system should provide relevant and explainable recommendations rather than simple keyword matches.
